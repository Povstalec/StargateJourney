# Stargate Re-Leveled
[Stargate Re-Leveled](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/stargate-releveled) is designed around creativity and longevity, providing a tiered quest system that leads you through 5 different custom circuits and multiple coexisting mods.
Many creativity centered mods like Create: Steam and Rails, FramedBlocks, and Chipped/Chisel come together to allow long term evolving designs and builds.
Thousands of custom recipes, items, structures, and integrations between mods create a truly unique experience found in no other Stargate centered modpack.

# Progression
The custom recipes and items available in this modpack are central to the tech progression and development of higher resources. Build factories to manufacture items you need to craft the next tier of circuit.
Mystical Agriculture integration provides easy to craft cruxes that are required to grow resources and automate more production lines.
Endgame materials require Oritech machines (new items, recipes, and circuits are still WIP)

# World Generation
Many new structures are added and modified from the Stargate Journey mod to better integrate into the pack.
All Stargate Journey Dimensions have been revamped to lower the bedrock to -64 and raise the height to match vanilla defaults.
Some Stargate Journey Dimensions have new modded ores included in them to provide more variety.

# Server Files
Server files are usually available on Curseforge. Servers created with these files have Common Stargates enabled by default, where the single player games will not.
